Three Distinct Classes

Choose your path and specialize your stats, gear enhancements, and combat buffs to match your playstyle.

Melee & Vanguard

Warrior

Frontline juggernauts clad in heavy armor. Warriors control the pace of battle with punishing close-quarters strikes, high durability, and shield-bearing resilience.

Ranged & Precision

Ranger

Agile marksmen and scouts built for mobility and lethal accuracy. Rangers pick off high-value targets from afar and control terrain with rapid skirmishing tactics.

Arcane & Elemental

Magi

Masters of destructive sorcery and tactical enchantments. Magi unleash devastating area spells and potent buffs while relying on positioning to outmaneuver foes.

Open World & Progression

Explore, gather, and grow stronger alone or alongside allies in a persistent fantasy realm.

Quests

Take on story-driven tasks, regional bounties, and perilous hunts across the realm to earn experience, gold, and rare equipment.

Crafting & Enhancement

Gather raw materials from the wilds to forge weapons, armor, and consumables, then enhance your gear and layer buffs for peak combat readiness.

Clans & Friends

Build your roster with an integrated friends list, form player clans, coordinate group runs, and rally your banner in both PvE and PvP.

PvP Battlegrounds

Test your build against other players in fast, arena-style modes powered by Sauerbraten's responsive movement and combat.

Capture the Flag

Twin Keeps

Storm opposing fortifications across a symmetrical stronghold valley, breach the inner sanctum, and escort the enemy flag home under fire.

King of the Hill

Riverbend

Fight for control of contested ground along a rugged river crossing, holding the capture zone against relentless waves of enemy attackers.

Team Deathmatch

Team Fight to the Finish

Pure squad-versus-squad warfare. Coordinate class synergies, focus priority targets, and outlast the rival team until the final blow.
